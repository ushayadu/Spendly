Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the app, we collect the expense data you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information or sensitive financial account details.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly service. This includes displaying your expense history, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and maintaining your account. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties for commercial purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Your account is protected by the password you set, and you are responsible for keeping it confidential.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for infrastructure and analytics (such as hosting providers). These services are bound by their own privacy policies and are not permitted to use your data for any purpose beyond supporting the delivery of Spendly. We do not integrate with external financial institutions or payment processors.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request deletion of your data, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app. We aim to respond to all privacy-related requests within 7 business days.
